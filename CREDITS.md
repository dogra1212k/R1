The images listed above are in `assets/`. They come from the corresponding project pages and have been resized and compressed into WebP; the app crops them for display. The individual creators retain copyright. Original project pages and license links are also available from the app's **Film credits** dialog.

Agent 327 has different film terms from the general Studio license. Its official player is embedded unchanged and its artwork is not included here. See the [creator's clarification](https://studio.blender.org/blog/agent-327-film-file-released-as-cc-by-nd/). Sprite Fright's own licensing page specifies its project terms; the catalog links directly to that notice instead of assigning the general Studio license version.

Blender's [remixing page](https://studio.blender.org/remixing/) describes the general attribution policy; individual asset notices and project exceptions take precedence. Logos and trademarks are not granted by these content licenses.

## R1 assets

`assets/icon.svg`, `assets/icon-192.png` and `assets/icon-512.png` are the R1 app icons created for this project. The interface uses original inline SVG icons. No Netflix logo, commercial movie posters or commercial movie files are included.

## Adding your collection

Keep each movie's creator credit, license, license link and source page accurate. Adding an HTTPS link does not establish streaming or redistribution rights. Use the Studio form to record the terms for content you own or have permission to use.
